Accounts

Professionals and students create an account with an email address and a password. If you forget your password, you can reset it from the log-in screen using a link sent to your email. Each professional account also has a public booking code you choose; it appears in your booking link and QR code and is not something you log in with.

Clients do not need a password. A client's account is identified by a personal code, which clients can optionally back up by adding a recovery email address; if you forget your code, we can email it to that address. If no recovery email was added, a lost code cannot be recovered, so please keep it somewhere you'll remember.

You are responsible for keeping your log-in details and code private and for activity under your account. You may delete your account at any time from the Account screen, which permanently removes your data and frees your code for someone else to use.

Using the app

All Hair Connect lets professionals track client history and technique notes ("Hair Passport"), manage bookings, and optionally list themselves publicly on the Find a Pro directory. Clients can look up and book with listed professionals, and both sides can view their own booked appointments. Use of the app is intended for licensed and student barbering/cosmetology professionals and their adult clients.

Notifications

Turning it on means you consent to receive appointment-related notifications sent through All Hair Connect. Notification frequency depends on your own appointment activity; this is not a marketing program.
