Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

All Hair Connect ("we," "us," or "the app") provides a booking, client-record, and communication tool for barbers, cosmetologists, and their clients. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect the name and personal code you choose. Licensed professionals may also add a license number and, if they opt in to public listing, business contact details shown on the Find a Pro directory. Professionals also enter details about services, hours, and clients they serve, as part of using the app.

Both professionals and clients may optionally turn on push notifications. Turning this on registers your browser or device to receive quick notifications about a specific booked appointment; no phone number is collected for this, and it is never shown to the other party.

When a booking is made, we store appointment details: date, time, services, and price.

How we use information

  • To operate the app's core features: scheduling, client records ("Hair Passport"), and the Find a Pro directory.
  • To send appointment-related push notifications, only when a user chooses to send one, or when a professional books an appointment on a client's behalf, and only to devices that have turned notifications on.
  • To maintain the accuracy and security of accounts.
Notification data. If you turn on notifications, your browser or device registers a subscription that is used solely to deliver messages related to your booked appointments. It is never shared with, or made visible to, the other party to the appointment (the client cannot see the professional's subscription details, and the professional cannot see the client's). This information won't be shared with any third parties beyond the notification delivery providers needed to send it (such as Apple, Google, or Mozilla's push services, depending on your browser or device).

Notification opt-in details

Turning on notifications is always optional. By turning it on, you consent to receive push notifications related to appointments you have booked through All Hair Connect. Notification frequency varies based on your own appointment activity and is not a recurring marketing program. You may opt out at any time from your Account screen or from your browser or device's notification settings.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share notification subscription details between the two parties to an appointment. Information is shared only with the service providers that keep the app running (our hosting and database providers, and the push notification services built into your browser or device, solely to transmit messages you've asked to be sent), and only as needed to operate the service.

Data retention and security

Account and appointment information is kept for as long as an account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time from the Account screen. We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored information, though no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

All Hair Connect is intended for licensed and student barbering/cosmetology professionals and their adult clients, and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
